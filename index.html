About Me

I'm a robotics and IoT engineer based in Cairo, Egypt, with hands-on experience across the full engineering stack — from sensor wiring and power system design through embedded firmware to cloud-connected telemetry pipelines.

In robotics, I work with ROS/ROS2, Gazebo simulation, and autonomous navigation on platforms ranging from AVR and STM32 microcontrollers to Raspberry Pi and Linux edge computers. In IoT, I architect end-to-end systems from edge device to AWS cloud, using MQTT, REST, and WebSockets across platform-agnostic firmware.

Beyond engineering, I lead technical training programmes — managing instructors, designing curricula, and coaching competition teams. I was selected for the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) across all three phases, and have instructed over 10,000 students in robotics and programming.

  • Architected an end-to-end IoT data path for a three-node marine monitoring system spanning AWS and on-prem servers
  • Delivered two complete robot platforms end-to-end — autonomous ground robot and underwater ROV
  • Built production Qt5/PyQt5 real-time monitoring stations on two deployed systems
  • Published in the Journal of Advanced Technology in Applied Sciences (JATAS)
  • Awarded 2nd place at IEEE RAS as team leader for control-system architecture
  • Selected for DEPI Phases 1, 2, and 3
